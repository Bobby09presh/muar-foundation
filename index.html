WHO WE ARE

We Believe Everyone Deserves A Better Future.

MUAR Foundation is a non-governmental organization dedicated to creating positive change and improving the lives of people in underserved communities.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and communities through education, health support and sustainable development.

Our Vision

A world where everyone has equal opportunities to live a healthy, safe and productive life.

Our Objectives

Promote education, reduce poverty, improve healthcare and encourage community participation.
